Privacy Policy

Cut The Pauses: Jump Cut Editor · Effective October 30, 2026

The short version: Cut The Pauses does not collect, store or share your personal information. Your videos, recordings and scripts stay on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

This policy explains how the Cut The Pauses app ("the app", "we") handles information. The app is made by Bradley White.

Information we collect

None. We do not run servers that receive your data, and the app has no sign-in, advertising, analytics or tracking tools. We never sell or share information about you.

Your videos, audio and scripts

Videos you record or choose, voice-overs, music you add, scripts you write and your drafts are stored only on your device, inside the app. Editing, finding pauses and finding filler words like "um" all happen on your device. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera and Microphone: to record videos and voice-overs in the app.
  • Photos: to open the videos you choose, save your edited videos, and (only if you ask) delete a draft's original clips.
  • Speech Recognition: to find filler words and retakes and to follow your script in the teleprompter. The app asks your iPhone to do this on the device. On older devices that can't recognize speech on the device, Apple's speech recognition may process the audio on Apple's servers, under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive it.

You can change these at any time in your device's Settings › CTP.

Speech model downloads

The first time you open the app, it downloads speech models from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) so it can understand speech on your device. This is a standard file download: no personal information, videos or audio are sent. Like any website, the download server can see your device's IP address when the files are requested.

Feedback emails

If you choose to send feedback from the app, it opens an email in your Mail app with your app version, device model, iOS version, free storage space and a short list of recent app problems already filled in. You can read and change it before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send it. We use these emails only to answer you and fix problems, and we don't share them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your name, email address or payment details. The app only learns from Apple whether a subscription is active.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email CutThePauses@gmail.com.
