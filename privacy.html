Privacy Policy

Cut The Pauses: Jump Cut Editor · Effective November 2, 2026

The short version: Cut The Pauses does not collect, store or share your personal information. Your videos, recordings and scripts stay on your device. There are no accounts and no ads. On Android, two Google tools built into the app send Google anonymous usage reports (never your videos): see On Android.

This policy explains how the Cut The Pauses app ("the app", "we") handles information. The app is made by Bradley White.

Information we collect

None. We do not run servers that receive your data, and the app has no sign-in or advertising. We never sell or share information about you. The iPhone app has no analytics tools; the Android app includes Google tools that send Google anonymous usage reports, described in On Android.

Your videos, audio and scripts

Videos you record or choose, voice-overs, music you add, scripts you write and your drafts are stored only on your device, inside the app. Editing, finding pauses and finding filler words like "um" all happen on your device. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera and Microphone: to record videos and voice-overs in the app.
  • Photos: to open the videos you choose, save your edited videos, and (only if you ask) delete a draft's original clips.
  • Speech Recognition: to find filler words and retakes and to follow your script in the teleprompter. The app asks your iPhone to do this on the device. On older devices that can't recognize speech on the device, Apple's speech recognition may process the audio on Apple's servers, under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive it.

You can change these at any time in your device's Settings › CTP.

Speech model downloads

The first time you open the app, it downloads speech models from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) so it can understand speech on your device. This is a standard file download: no personal information, videos or audio are sent. Like any website, the download server can see your device's IP address when the files are requested.

Feedback emails

If you choose to send feedback from the app, it opens an email in your Mail app with your app version, device model, iOS version, free storage space and a short list of recent app problems already filled in. You can read and change it before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send it. We use these emails only to answer you and fix problems, and we don't share them.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your name, email address or payment details. The app only learns from Apple whether a subscription is active.

On Android

The Android version works the same way, with these differences:

  • Face and person detection: to zoom toward your face, keep you in frame and cut you out for green screen, the app uses Google's ML Kit, which runs on your phone. Your videos and pictures never leave the phone. ML Kit sends Google anonymous usage reports so Google can measure and improve it: your phone's model and Android version, the app's name and version, a random identifier for this install (not meant to identify you or your phone), and speed and error figures. Google says it doesn't pass this to third parties; see Google's ML Kit data disclosure. We never receive it.
  • Teleprompter listening: the app asks your phone to recognize speech on the device. On phones that can't do that, Android's speech recognition may process the audio on Google's servers, under Google's Privacy Policy. We never receive it.
  • Permissions: Camera and Microphone to record; Notifications to show progress while videos are made in the background. Videos are opened with Android's own picker, so the app only sees the ones you choose.
  • Purchases: subscriptions are handled entirely by Google Play through your Google account. We never see your name, email address or payment details.
  • Feedback emails: open in your own email app, with your app version, phone model, Android version, free storage and a short list of recent app problems filled in; nothing is sent unless you send it.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
