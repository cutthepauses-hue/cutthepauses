Common questions

Why does the app download files the first time I open it?
Cut The Pauses downloads its speech models once so it can find ums and retakes right on your iPhone. Leave the app open on Wi-Fi for a few minutes the first time.

Pauses are being missed, or too much is cut.
In the editor, adjust how tight the edit is, or change the quiet level and shortest pause to cut. Your exported video always matches the preview.

The app can't see my videos or camera.
Open your iPhone's Settings › CTP and allow Camera, Microphone, Photos and Speech Recognition as needed.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?
Open your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Choose Cut The Pauses Pro to change or cancel it. To restore a subscription on a new device, use Restore Purchases in the app.

How do I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
